Countdown & Habit Tracker

A simple way to notice the days that matter.

Status: Beta
  • iOS
  • Widgets
  • Time

Countdowns, "days since", progress through the year and small habits, shown mostly in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Why I started

Calendars are good at telling you what’s next and bad at helping you feel time passing. I wanted something that sits quietly on a Home Screen and keeps a handful of meaningful dates and habits in view.

What it does

  • Countdowns to the dates you choose, repeating every year or month if you like.
  • Days since something happened.
  • Progress through the year, month, week, day or a range you set.
  • Habits, counted against a daily, weekly or monthly goal, with streaks. Log one from the widget without opening the app.
  • Shows each one as a number, percentage, dots, ring, bar or calendar, with preset or custom themes, in the app and in widgets.

Status: Beta

Available in beta for iPhone, by invitation only. Get in touch if you'd like to be part of the beta.
