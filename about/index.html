About

Hello, I'm Francesco.

I have spent much of my career working at the intersection of engineering, people, and complex organizations. I began with code and architecture. Over time, my work evolved toward leading teams and helping others make great things.

From architecture to leadership

I started as a client-facing solutions architect, moved on to lead enterprise architecture, and later headed engineering organizations. Over more than fifteen years I've helped design, build and run large-scale digital products for consumers, businesses, industry and government, with teams and clients spread across Europe, the Americas and Asia-Pacific.

For several years I led the digital touchpoints of one of the largest utility companies in the world, with a portfolio serving both consumers and businesses, a team of more than fifty people, and the kind of budget where every decision has consequences. I've interviewed hundreds of candidates along the way, and I've learned at least as much from them as they did from me.

Today I work as an AI and engineering leader, helping people and organizations figure out what this technology actually changes, and what it doesn't.

Why I never stopped building

I love making things. AI has made that part of my life unexpectedly exciting again. Building my own apps keeps me grounded as a leader. It reminds me what the work actually feels like, where the tools help and where they get in the way. Leading teams changes how I build. I care more about the problem than the cleverness of the solution.

Why I write

To me, writing is a process of self-discovery. It's how I work out what I think. Monday Muse started as practical notes on leadership and career growth. It has slowly become a place to ask bigger questions about the value of human judgment, creativity, and attention when machines can do more and more of what we used to call our work.

Beyond work

I take photographs, mostly of my family and travels. I read just about anything: philosophy, history, the occasional novel that rearranges something in my head. I work hard to protect enough unstructured time to get bored and notice things.

What I believe about leadership

Leadership is about creating an environment built on trust and real autonomy, then getting out of the way. Coaching and mentoring matter enormously. Some of the most rewarding conversations of my career have been with people figuring out what's next. If that's you, here's how I can help.

Short bio

Francesco Fusco is an AI and engineering leader with more than fifteen years in the technology industry. He began his career as a solutions architect, became a lead enterprise architect, and went on to head engineering organizations building large-scale digital products for consumers, businesses and public-sector clients across Europe, the Americas and Asia-Pacific. Alongside his day job he builds independent software and writes Monday Muse, a newsletter on technology, leadership and what it means to be human in the age of AI.
