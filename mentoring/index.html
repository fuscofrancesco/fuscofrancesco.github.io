Mentoring

Helping engineers grow into the leaders they want to be.

Over more than fifteen years, I’ve hired, coached and learned from a lot of engineers and leaders. I offer 1:1 mentoring for people who want an honest, experienced perspective on their next step.

Start a conversation How it works

How I can help

  1. 01

    From engineer to manager

    Making the move from individual contributor to leading people: what changes, what to let go of, and how to earn trust in a new role.

  2. 02

    Leadership development

    The practical skills new and aspiring managers need: feedback, delegation, difficult conversations, managing up, and building a team people want to stay in.

  3. 03

    Leading through AI

    How AI is changing engineering work, teams and careers. And how to lead people through that change with clarity.

  4. 04

    Career direction

    Step-by-step planning for people starting out in tech, or switching into it from another field: skills to build, where to focus, and how to grow a network.

  5. 05

    Interview preparation

    Technical and behavioural interviews, with mock sessions and candid feedback from someone who has sat on the other side of the table more than a hundred times.

  6. 06

    System design reviews

    A second pair of eyes on your architecture: trade-offs, scalability, performance and the questions a good reviewer will ask.

Who I usually work with

  • Software engineers who want to move into management
  • Engineering managers who want to become better leaders
  • People in tech who feel stuck and want to get back on track
  • Students and career-changers getting started in the industry

How it works

  1. 1

    Say hello

    Send me a short note about where you are and what you’d like to work on.

  2. 2

    A first conversation

    A free 15-minute call to see whether I’m the right person to help. No obligation either way.

  3. 3

    Work together

    1:1 sessions, shaped around your goals. We agree on format and cadence together. One session is fine, and so is an ongoing rhythm.

I mentor alongside my day job, so availability varies. If I can’t take something on, I’ll tell you honestly and, where I can, point you to someone who can.
